## What is Logic Pro?

Logic Pro is Apple's flagship digital audio workstation, the professional step up from GarageBand. The current version, Logic Pro 11, records audio and MIDI, hosts software instruments and plug-ins, and mixes complete productions, all in one app. It is used by songwriters, producers, and composers, and it stays a one-time purchase rather than a subscription, with Apple delivering major updates for free since 2013.

Logic Pro runs on Mac and iPad, and the Mac version is deeply optimized for Apple silicon.

## AI studio assistant features

Logic Pro 11 added a set of AI-powered tools that speed up music-making. Session Players expand the popular Drummer with a new Bass Player and Keyboard Player that follow your chords. Stem Splitter extracts individual parts — vocals, drums, bass, and other — from a single stereo recording. ChromaGlow adds analog warmth, and the Mastering Assistant helps finish a track. These tools augment your work while leaving you in full creative control.

## A complete studio

Logic Pro includes a large collection of instruments, effects, loops, and samples — over 70 GB of sound content — so you can produce entire tracks without third-party plug-ins. Live Loops lets you build music non-linearly from cells, the Tracks view handles traditional arrangement, and a professional mixer with plug-ins covers mixing and mastering.

## Dolby Atmos and spatial audio

Logic Pro supports Dolby Atmos mixing, so you can create immersive spatial audio productions directly in the app. Combined with its surround and stereo tools, that makes Logic Pro capable of delivering music in the formats modern releases and streaming platforms use.

## Pricing

Logic Pro for Mac is a one-time purchase of $199.99 on the Mac App Store, with no subscription and free major updates. It is also available in Apple's Creator Studio subscription bundle alongside Final Cut Pro and other Apple pro apps. There is no separate trial for the Mac version, though Apple offers ways to evaluate its pro apps.
